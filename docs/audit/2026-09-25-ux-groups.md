# UX Audit — Groups (UX-GRP-01..15)

Transcribed from `UX-Audit-Groups.docx.pdf`, section "10. Groups". 15 items.

Status: **transcribed and planned.** See `2026-09-25-ux-groups-plan.md` for the pull requests and the
decisions taken with the product owner on 2026-09-25, which are not to be re-litigated.

The audit's framing: groups live inside communities and inherit their roles and permissions (the
permissions matrix is in `2026-09-14-ux-community.md`). There is no group-level admin role; the admins of
a community administer its groups, and promotion happens at community level. **Decision 1 in the plan
narrows this for private groups** — a community admin manages a private group only as a member.

## Items

**UX-GRP-01 — Create Group.** Task flow, navbar hidden. Only for users in a community where they are admin
or have the create-groups permission; otherwise the action is absent. Entry points, all "Create group":
Your Groups, top of the community Groups tab, Manage Groups. Header: ✕ left, centred title, divider; ✕
opens a discard sheet, no drafts. Community selector only from Your Groups and only with more than one
eligible community, as a bottom-sheet dropdown. Form: name, description, thumbnail (preset row + upload,
change/remove top-right; no cover), Private toggle in a card ("only invited players can see the group").
Primary button fixed at the bottom, disabled until required fields are filled. On success the group
opens with a temporary banner. Default group: created with the community, public, named
"<community> Group"; behaves like any other (rename, edit, private, leave). A community is never left
without a group: archiving is blocked for whichever group is the last active one.

**UX-GRP-02 — Join Group.** Two privacy levels, no request-to-join. Header: back, thumbnail, name,
description subtitle; overlapping avatars + player count on one line. Public: visible to all community
members, all content visible before joining (Events row + See all, Ranking + See all, General info with
the community card and creation date), fixed "Join Group", immediate. Private: invisible to uninvited
community members (Groups tab and search), visible in Your Groups for members; reached only through an
invitation notification; preview shows "Private Group" label + explanation, avatars and count, community
card, creation date — no events or ranking; inviter photo and name above Decline (secondary) / Accept
(primary). After joining: full screen + banner; "+ Invite members" in the header when permitted; the
"Private Group" label stays.

**UX-GRP-03 — Your Groups.** One list, sectioned by community, no tabs. Header: back, "Your Groups",
"+" to Create Group (replacing "Explore groups"), plus a "Create group" action below — both only when
eligible. Section header: community name (smaller than the title) + "Show all"; below, a horizontal row
of cards with a small messaging-style thumbnail, name and description. Archived groups: kept for admins
with an "Archived" tag, invisible to members; opening one is read-only with a fixed "Unarchive group" and a
confirm sheet. Empty: standard empty state, CTA to Explore with the Groups tab selected.

**UX-GRP-04 — Group Detail.** Header: back, small thumbnail, name, description subtitle; "⋯" for members,
settings icon for admins; avatars + count with "+ Invite members" on the right of the same line. Remove
the centred identity block, the duplicated count, and "Abrir conversa". Events: horizontal row + "See all"
on the title line, no "Create event" row; empty state with "Create event" when there are none. Ranking:
no period pills until there is content; "Last update {DATE}" below the title, "See all" on the title line;
top 10 as a table (position, avatar, name, Points / Win / Lost) with a sort control; empty state with a
create-event CTA. Past seasons section at the bottom, hidden when none. General info: community card with
thumbnail, name and chevron; creation date.

**UX-GRP-05 — Group Events.** From "See all". Back + "Events". Upcoming / Past tabs, Upcoming default.
Full-width horizontal cards, inline actions where the user can act. "Create event" at the top. Empty state
per tab.

**UX-GRP-06 — Group Ranking.** From "See all". Back + "Ranking", "Last update {DATE}". Table: position,
avatar, name, Points, Events played, sort control. Row → player profile. Departed players in greyscale
with a "no longer in group" tag. Fixed "Share" (native share sheet).

**UX-GRP-07 — Members.** Back + "Members". Search at the top; full-width "Invite member" below it when
permitted. Rows: photo, name, chevron. Admin tap → member actions sheet (GRP-13); member tap → profile.
Departed players greyscale with a tag.

**UX-GRP-08 — Invite Members.** Follows community permissions (UX-COMM-17). ✕ left, centred title,
divider. "Share" and "Copy Link" side by side, an "or" divider, then search. Fixed confirm showing the
count. Results grouped: Community members, then My connections (people the user follows); anyone else by
searching their name. Multi-select. If the selection includes people outside the community, confirming
opens "Invite members to group & community" explaining they will be added to both; accepting adds them to
both in one action. Otherwise invites are sent directly.

**UX-GRP-09 — Group Actions.** App bottom sheet, ✕ top-right: Share (native share sheet), Leave Group
(GRP-15).

**UX-GRP-10 — Manage Group.** Bottom sheet "Manage Group", ✕ top-right, from the admin settings icon.
Rows with chevrons: Group Settings, Manage Members, Reset Ranking. Actions below: Share, Leave Group,
Archive Group (unavailable for the last active group, with a reason). Archiving is a group-level action,
not in Seasons.

**UX-GRP-11 — Group Settings.** Same form as Create Group, in a bottom sheet with ✕ top-right and a fixed
primary Save. No community selector — groups do not move between communities.

**UX-GRP-12 — Manage Members.** The same component as Members, reached from a second path. No inline
"Remover do grupo"; tap opens the member actions sheet; swipe exposes "Remove from group".

**UX-GRP-13 — Member Actions.** Bottom sheet with photo and name, ✕ top-right: See profile; Remove from
group (this group only, stays in the community; confirm sheet). No "Make admin" — that lives in the
community's Manage Members (UX-COMM-20). Members without management permission go straight to the
profile.

**UX-GRP-14 — Seasons and Reset Ranking.** "Reset ranking" from Manage Group opens a confirm sheet
directly: the current season closes, everyone restarts at zero, nothing is lost (the season stays as a
past season with its ranking and match history). Reset and new season are the same action. The admin then
sees a completion screen (closing message, final leaderboard, Share); every other member sees a
season-ended notice with the final standings the next time they open the group. Past seasons: cards at
the bottom of the group page with title and period, each opening that season's final ranking and events;
hidden when none. The standalone Seasons screen goes. Archive moves to Manage Group, with a confirm sheet
stating that upcoming events are cancelled; unavailable for the last active group.

**UX-GRP-15 — Leave Group.** Check the condition before confirming, never after. Last admin: a sheet
explaining another admin is needed first, primary action to resolve it, secondary Cancel — never an
error. Everyone else: confirm sheet saying leaving removes access to the group's information; match
records are not deleted. After leaving: still visible in the member list, ranking and past events in
greyscale with a "no longer in group" tag; rejoining in the same season restores points; rejoining in a
new season starts at zero; a player who left still appears in that season's final ranking.
